Fotune: Album Search

Find albums by place & people.

Fotune finds albums, not just photos. Type what you remember — a place, a year, the people you were with, part of an album name — all at once, like “Jeju 2024 Mom”, and the matching albums show up.

  • One search box for album names, places, years and people
  • Browse albums by place on a map, by date, and by the people you were with
  • Everything is indexed on your iPhone — your photos are never uploaded
