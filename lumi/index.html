Lumi - Photo Location & Geotag

Add GPS to camera & DSLR shots.

Photos from a DSLR, mirrorless, or digital camera have no GPS data, so they never show up on your map or in location searches. Lumi infers each photo's location from the visit and movement history recorded on your iPhone — plus the GPS of iPhone photos taken around the same time — and puts it back.

  • Detects and bulk-imports non-GPS camera photos, hundreds at once
  • Shows its evidence — visits, routes, nearby photos — with a confidence score
  • Never edits originals: creates a new file with the GPS written in
  • Location collection can be turned off and deleted anytime — no account required
